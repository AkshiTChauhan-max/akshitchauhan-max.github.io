About Me

I am a Technical Support Engineer at Central Electronics Limited (CEL), Railway Business Division — a Government of India enterprise — where I work on installation, commissioning, calibration and maintenance of SSDAC / DAC axle counters and High Speed Axle Detectors used by Indian Railways. I hold a Diploma in Electronics Engineering (CRRIT, Delhi, 2022). Alongside my field work, I design and build my own Android applications from scratch — plain Java, no AndroidX, no third-party dependencies — focused on solving real problems I face in my daily work: PDF tools for railway tickets and forms, a document scanner, a train information app, and a cleaner that understands modern Android and cloned apps.
